The craft

Measured here.
Finished by hand.

We select every fabric with care — crisp cotton, easy blends and festive silks — and bring it home to Bodinayakanur, where each piece is measured, cut and finished by hand. Nothing leaves the atelier until the fall sits right and the collar holds its shape.

For the festive season we keep the workshop open late — so a shirt bought on a Tuesday can be altered and ready by the weekend.

  • Hand-finishedButtons, seams and hems finished in-house.
